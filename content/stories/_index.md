These are in varying states of completeness; some are more fragmentary than others. Some began as dreams, some as imaginings, some as things that happened, and I am not going to tell you which — partly because I could not reliably do it. A story is an articulated dream, never fully separated from life.

Some run to a single line. Length is not what makes a story.

Let me know what you think.
