title: "An imaginary university"
date: 2004-08-08
draft: false
---

My vision of the ideal University is a place where people are not preoccupied with numbers and reports. I want to work in a place where people knew what they were employed to do and were given adequate support to help them achieve it. I imagine a university where the bureaucratic systems are designed to support the income-generating work, rather than the other way around. I envisage a place that does not spend endless resources on re-designing its logo or re-organizing its structure or its name to take advantage of some temporary perception of market advantage. In an ideal University, I would not have the feeling that *the bureaucrats have taken over the asylum*. I want to work in a place that frequently and sincerely uses vocabulary like "academic freedom", "academic and non-academic incentivization of work", "scholarship", rather than bandying them around as platitudes.

In short, I would like to be in a university that has a value system more geared up to academic and scholarly excellence, than to pseudo-industrial bean-counting exercises.
