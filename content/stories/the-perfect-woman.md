---
title: "The perfect woman"
date: 2003-01-01
draft: false
---

I didn't recall how we had met her. Just that she had visited once before. I had completely forgotten about her until I saw her walking brightly up our driveway, looking fit and healthy, exuding positive energy, eyes bright and somehow fully engaged with all her senses. She saw me noticing her through the window. So, instead of waiting for her knock, I went straight to the front door to open it as she arrived.

Despite the intervening years since we'd last met, the eye contact was immediately electrical. She was simply and naturally beautiful. Huge smile, sparkly eyes, and arms instinctively raising for a hug, I stepped forward to reciprocate. We hugged like familiar lives, hands moving over each others’ backs as I emulated her enthusiastic moves, bordering on the intimate but crossing no lines of decency.

As my wife came to see who had arrived, I released the hug and turned to face her, as our visitor maintained one arm casually around my waist a slight moment too long and we also said our hellos, my wife being as happy to see her as I was. She stepped across lightly and hugged my wife hello like a long lost friend.

She was such a complete and natural person, that there was no threat to my marriage. She was not coquettish, though she was close to it. She could have wrapped me round her little finger, but she didn't need to. And I didn't need to be hers. It was enough to spend time with her, to see her and my wife also enjoying each other’s company and to welcome her as a friend, fully and honestly.
